How we teach

We show you how to teach it

Most practice books hand parents a page of questions and nothing else. When your child gets stuck, it’s hard to know what to say, and practice time can turn into a standoff. Every lesson we make comes with a short parent script, so you know exactly how to help.

What’s in a parent script

  • Words to say. Plain, friendly language you can read straight from the page.
  • Show first, then practise together. You model the skill, try it side by side, then hand it over.
  • What to do when they’re stuck. A hint to give before the answer, so your child does the thinking.
  • Short sessions. Most lessons take 10 to 15 minutes, so practice fits around family life.

Why it works

Children learn a new skill best when someone shows them first, practises with them, and then steps back. Teachers often call this “I do, we do, you do.” Our scripts bring that same approach to the kitchen table.

You don’t need to be a teacher. You just need to know what to say next.
