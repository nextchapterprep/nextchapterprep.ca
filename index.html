The application year at a glance

For children starting Grade 7 in fall 2027, the application happens this school year, while they are in Grade 6.

  1. September to November

    Visit and shortlist

    Go to open houses with your child, take notes, and narrow your list to the schools that suit them.

  2. Late November to January

    Apply

    Many day schools share a December 1 deadline; others fall between late November and January. Ask teachers for references early, and start the financial aid application at the same time.

  3. Fall to winter

    Tests and interviews

    Many schools use the SSAT, with sittings through the fall and winter, and some run their own assessments. Interviews and visit days often begin in the fall.

  4. Late February

    Offers

    Many schools release decisions on a shared offer date in late February. Then comes the choice your family makes together.

As of fall 2026. Dates, tests and requirements vary from school to school, so check each school's admissions page for its own.
