Workbooks

Workbooks on the way

Practice books families can use at home, with a parent script for every lesson. We’re starting with the two years before Grade 7, and more grades will follow.

Grade 5

Build

Reading, writing and math practice matched to the Ontario curriculum, plus a gentle first look at how independent schools work.

  • A parent script for every lesson
  • Writing practice that builds week by week
  • Short sessions of 10 to 15 minutes
Grade 6

Apply

The application year, step by step: choosing schools, tests, interviews and the parent statement.

  • A month-by-month family checklist
  • Interview practice in your child’s own words
  • Help with the parent statement
