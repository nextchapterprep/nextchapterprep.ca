Learning by grade

Learning at every stage

What children need changes as they grow. Here’s where we focus at each stage, matched to the Ontario curriculum.

Kindergarten to Grade 2

Strong foundations

Early reading and writing, number sense, and the curiosity and listening skills that make school feel good from the start.

What we work on
  • Letter sounds and blending
  • Counting, adding and subtracting
  • Listening and following steps
Grades 3 and 4

Building fluency

Reading to learn, writing clear paragraphs, and confident work with multiplication, fractions and word problems.

What we work on
  • Reading for meaning
  • Writing a clear paragraph
  • Multiplication and fractions
Grades 5 and 6

Ready for what’s next

Longer writing, multi-step problem solving and good study habits, plus guidance for families applying to independent schools.

What we work on
  • Longer writing
  • Multi-step problems
  • Study habits and staying calm on tests
